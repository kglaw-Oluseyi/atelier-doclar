# EOS-S05B AI Safety Scoring and Evaluation

**Status:** CEO review draft

## Permitted AI roles

Document classification, cited field proposals, contradiction detection, renewal summaries, gap explanations, continuity options, incident chronology draft and executive briefing draft.

## Prohibited AI roles

No legal conclusion, coverage guarantee, policy verification, protected-trait inference, vendor approval/rejection, contract approval, residual-risk acceptance, fallback authorisation, reserve authority, claim submission, external message, booking or payment.

## Risk intelligence

Use a deterministic model edition for computed bands. AI can narrate the already-computed factors but cannot secretly alter weights. Display unknowns, counter-evidence, recency and materiality. Staff can override only through an explicit, audited decision that preserves the computed result.

## Prompt-injection and file safety

Uploaded text is untrusted content. Provider prompts delimit it as data. Tool use is allowlisted. URLs, instructions, hidden markup and macros found in documents are inert. Output is schema-validated, escaped and citation-bound.

## Executable evaluation

Create `s05b-eval-v1` with real production functions, isolated synthetic snapshots and persisted per-case results. Minimum families:

- organisation/event/cross-organisation isolation;
- policy applicability and unknown inputs;
- expiry and stale legal rule;
- conflicting certificates;
- OCR fabrication negative control;
- venue-rule evidence and supersession;
- residual-risk maker/checker;
- clause legal-review gate;
- vendor indicator explainability and trait negative control;
- missed check-in and escalation without dispatch;
- fallback proposal without booking/payment;
- reserve scenario trace and no invented prices;
- incident chronology truth;
- restricted document projection/export;
- dossier immutability and no dispatch;
- prompt injection and malicious markup;
- concurrency, idempotency and false-success;
- Unicode/Yorùbá integrity;
- accessibility and responsive semantics.

Negative adapters must prove the harness detects unsafe behaviour. Cases may not supply their own pass booleans. Corpus/version changes make prior passes `STALE`; missing case rows are `INCOMPATIBLE`; no run is `UNRUN`; all block release.
