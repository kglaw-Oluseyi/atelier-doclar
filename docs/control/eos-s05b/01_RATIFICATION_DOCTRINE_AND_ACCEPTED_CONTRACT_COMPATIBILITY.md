# EOS-S05B Ratification Doctrine and Accepted Contract Compatibility

**Status:** CEO review draft

## Mission

Build one organisation-wide and event-scoped protection system that makes exposure, evidence, accountability and continuity visible without creating a second event, vendor, budget, payment, communication or authority ledger.

## Binding principles

- Event OS remains operational truth.
- Organisation policy applicability is evaluated, versioned and snapshotted for an event; source policies are not copied into event truth.
- An event protection is never visible outside its organisation and event.
- Coverage, compliance, readiness, payment status and operational continuity are distinct products.
- A certificate is evidence, not proof that every claimed circumstance is covered.
- A policy marked active is not automatically applicable to a loss.
- Contract wording is a governed draft until legal approval; the system never describes an unenforceable penalty as enforceable.
- A fallback recommendation is not vendor engagement, booking, payment or reserve deployment.
- An emergency reserve is a Budget Intelligence allocation, not cash, authority or a payment instrument.
- AI may extract, compare, flag and recommend. It may not approve policies, clauses, vendors, fallbacks, claims, money movements or governing facts.

## Accepted-contract integration

| Accepted truth | EOS-S05B obligation |
|---|---|
| `guestId` is a person | No risk entity may substitute vendor, household or invitation identity for a person. |
| Event scope enforced | Every event record carries organisation and event scope; organisation records use explicit applicability. |
| S03 quantity authority | Risk calculations consume governed quantities and never replace RSVP or entitlement truth. |
| Programme phases canonical | Exposure is phase-aware and never naively sums phase populations. |
| S05 spatial truth | Venue zones, restricted areas, assets and layouts are referenced permission-safely; no parallel geometry. |
| S05A Budget Intelligence | Risk allowances and reserves are immutable scenario drivers with trace; no parallel budget ledger. |
| Communications governed | Reminder drafts enter the communications outbox; no silent Brevo/Twilio dispatch. |
| Maker/checker | Consequential decisions require distinct authorised people, server enforced. |
| Concurrency/idempotency | Durable commands require expected versions and stable idempotency keys. |
| Append-only audit | Every decision, denial and external effect is correlated and immutable. |
| Editions immutable | Approved policy/clause/dossier editions supersede through lineage. |
| No trait inference | Risk scoring cannot infer protected or culturally sensitive traits. |

## Pushback on supplied assumptions

The following are catalogue examples, not defaults:

- “5% emergency reserve”; 
- “20–40% retainer”; 
- “150–200% liquidated damages”; 
- specimen premiums or coverage limits;
- named-venue insurance requirements;
- fixed 72/24/6 hour check-ins.

Each becomes a versioned policy rule with jurisdiction, event type, criticality, evidence, effective dates, review status and authorised approver. The UI may recommend a range only when its provenance and status are visible.

## Non-goals

No insurer portal, binding quote, underwriting, legal advice, claim settlement, contract signature, payment, vendor booking, live reminder dispatch, emergency-service dispatch or public client access is authorised in this slice.
