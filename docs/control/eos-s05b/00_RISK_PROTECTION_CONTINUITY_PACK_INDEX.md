## Product conclusion

Risk must become an operational command capability, not a document cupboard. The system must answer five questions truthfully: what protection applies; what evidence supports it; what remains exposed; what must happen next; and who is authorised to decide. It must preserve uncertainty and never manufacture legal, insurance or financial certainty.
