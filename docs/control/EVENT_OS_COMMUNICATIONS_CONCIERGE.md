## What this slice owns

Event-scoped guest communications, synthetic delivery operations, and two-way concierge follow-up.

Staff may prepare policy and guest-safe occasion facts, version templates, build explainable audiences, freeze immutable snapshots at approval, enqueue synthetic delivery, project delivery truth, ingest synthetic inbound, and own concierge tasks.

## What this slice does not own

- Guest identity, intake fields, or RSVP response truth (S01–S03 remain controlling)
- RSVP invitation capability (S03 issues tokens; S04 may only deliver an already issued invitation)
- Seating, travel booking, accommodation booking, protocol/cultural judgement, credentials, check-in, admission, FaceGate, Event-Day runtime, Academy, Marketing, Ushering
- Production providers (Twilio, Meta WhatsApp, SendGrid, SES, or any live sender)
- Autonomous send, approve, suppress, VIP, or compensation decisions

## Contact model

S02 qualified email and phone remain source truth. S04 projects contact points for eligibility. Bounce, inbound, or signature inference never overwrites intake. Corrections are proposals that apply only through governed S02 amend semantics.
