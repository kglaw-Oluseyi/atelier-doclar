# EOS-S05A — Ratification, Architecture and Compatibility

Status: **CEO REVIEW DRAFT**

## 1. Purpose

Maison Doclar must understand the client before operational planning hardens assumptions into event truth. EOS-S05A creates the governed path from an initial human conversation to a confirmable Event Brief, investment advice, adaptive event roadmap, change-impact intelligence and Executive Event Command.

The client-facing promise is:

> A client will not be asked to repeat information because Maison Doclar lost it, failed to structure it, or failed to make it available to the right authorised person.

The promise does not prohibit confirmation when information conflicts, has aged, carries material risk, or would become binding.

## 2. Slice identity and sequencing

- Proposed ID: `EOS-S05A`.

## 3. Product doctrine

### Discovery doctrine

The conversation remains human; consequential meaning becomes structured. The system remembers sources, distinguishes fact from interpretation, exposes gaps and contradictions, and asks only the questions that remain useful.

### Investment doctrine

Maison Doclar does not classify a client's wealth. It records what the client wishes to invest, what the brief responsibly requires, what produces value, where restraint is welcome, who may approve expenditure and when commitments may arise.

### Roadmap doctrine

The roadmap is an executive abstraction of outcomes, decisions, dependencies, commitments and readiness—not a dump of internal tasks.

### Change doctrine

New information never silently rewrites governing truth. It produces a traceable proposal and impact assessment. Authorised humans decide whether and how affected records are superseded or rebaselined.

### AI doctrine

AI may listen, transcribe, extract, ask, compare, summarise, recommend and simulate consequences. AI may not confirm client truth, grant authority, approve expenditure, promise delivery, dispatch communications or mutate accepted downstream records without an authorised human action.

## 4. Architecture

The architecture has seven bounded capabilities:

1. **Engagement Intake** — enquiry/opportunity and prospective-client context before an operational Client/Event exists.
2. **Discovery Intelligence** — sessions, utterances, source artefacts, coverage, candidate assertions and contradictions.
3. **Canonical Event Brief** — immutable editions containing confirmed governing assertions and explicit unknowns.
4. **Investment Intelligence** — envelopes, scope boundaries, priorities, scenarios, recommendations, approvals and financial-state declarations.
5. **Roadmap Intelligence** — outcome milestones, dependencies, decisions, buffers, confidence, critical path and readiness.
6. **Change Intelligence** — proposed changes, affected contracts, staleness, consequence calculation, approval and rebaseline lineage.
7. **Executive Event Command** — permission-safe CEO exception view; supported by distinct planner and client projections.

## 5. Pre-engagement boundary

An exploratory interview must not automatically create an operational Event. The canonical hierarchy is extended, not replaced:

`Organisation → Engagement Opportunity → Discovery Engagement → Event Concept`

Following an authorised conversion decision:

`Event Concept → existing Client + existing Event + initial Event Brief edition`

Conversion must be idempotent and auditable. It must either link an existing client or create one under existing `client.create` authority. It must never create duplicate people or clients from approximate matches.

## 6. Accepted-contract compatibility

EOS-S05A shall reuse:

- existing Organisation, Client, Event, Person, assignment, role and permission truth;
- event scoping and least-privilege projections;
- append-only correlated audit;
- optimistic concurrency and idempotency;
- maker/checker for consequential approvals;
- immutable edition and supersession patterns;
- S03 invitation/RSVP quantity authority;
- distinct RSVP, forecast, provision and observed-attendance products;
- canonical programme phases;
- EOS-S05 venue/layout publication truth;
- governed communications with no silent dispatch;
- separate staff, private-host, guest and vendor sessions.

EOS-S05A must not create parallel versions of any of those ledgers.

## 7. Explicit prohibitions

- No wealth, status, ethnicity, religion, family role, gender, health or protected-trait inference.
- No inference of investment capacity from name, location, occupation, dress, family or social signals.
- No binding truth sourced solely from an LLM output.
- No autonomous client commitment, supplier commitment, payment, booking or communication.
- No AI-generated critical-path claim without deterministic evidence and disclosed assumptions.
- No task-count percentage used as the sole definition of event readiness.
- No naive summation of phase populations.
- No real data before separate real-client onboarding authority.
- No public transcript, artefact or model-provider URL.
- No secrets in prompts, logs, screenshots or evidence.

## 8. Non-goals for this slice

- General accounting or bank reconciliation.
- Payment execution, receipts, refunds or financial-instrument storage.
- Supplier contracting or provider booking.
- Autonomous pricing trained on ungoverned market data.
- External WhatsApp/email dispatch.
- Seating allocation (EOS-S06).
- Live event-day/offline command.
- Biometrics or protected-gate credentials.
- Programme Control Tower changes unless executable code is directly required.

## 9. Executive Event Command naming

Use these distinct names:

- **Programme Control Tower** — software/programme governance.
- **Executive Event Command** — CEO event and portfolio intelligence.
- **Planner Intelligence Workspace** — detailed operational preparation.
- **Client Roadmap** — calm, permission-safe client view.

## 10. Ratification test

Ratification is sound only if the implementation route can demonstrate:

- no important discovery content is lost in prose;
- AI proposals are visibly distinct from confirmed truth;
- a client can correct the brief;
- a later contradiction is detected without silent overwrite;
- investment products remain distinct;
- roadmap dependencies propagate consequences without unauthorised mutation;
- every readiness conclusion is explainable;
- every role sees only its minimum-necessary projection;
- the experience remains conversational and premium rather than questionnaire-driven.
