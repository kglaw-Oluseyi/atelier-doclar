# EOS-S05A — Independent Verification and Acceptance

## 1. Verification principle

Claude simulates a thoughtful client and independent human operator. Claude verifies; Claude never accepts. Functional automation remains authoritative for exhaustive matrices, concurrency and provider fixtures. Human verification concentrates on conversational quality, discoverability, trust, truthful state and changed role boundaries.

## 2. Deployment gate

Before testing, confirm exact expected deployed SHA, Railway `atelier-doclar/production/event-os`, live URL, health/readiness, POSTGRES, migrations APPLIED and `productionAuthorised:false`. Stop if the gate changes.

Synthetic state-changing actions enumerated in the final prompt are standing-authorised. George enters human-controlled secrets directly; Claude never reads or records them.

## 3. Efficient role coverage

### CEO — complete primary journey

- Create synthetic opportunity and discovery engagement.
- Conduct/resume an interview with contradictions and unknowns.
- Review source-linked assertions and coverage.
- Publish a brief through maker/checker using a second authorised role where required.
- Create investment scenarios including advice to spend less.
- Generate short-lead roadmap and inspect critical path.
- Introduce a material change and approve/reject propagation.
- Inspect Executive Event Command and client projection.

### Planner — authoring delta

- Can capture/review/propose within scope.
- Cannot self-approve consequential editions.
- Can understand gaps, sources and next action.

### Event Director — checker delta

- Reviews exact editions/hashes and impact.
- Can approve only granted decisions.
- Does not gain CEO-only portfolio or financial authority implicitly.

### Auditor — evidence delta

- Read-only controls absent.
- Provenance and decisions intelligible.
- sensitive content masked;
- direct mutation/API attempts denied without false success.

### Client — separate-session delta

- Can understand disclosure/consent.
- Can pause/resume, correct, defer and confirm permitted assertions.
- Cannot see staff notes, other clients, internal confidence or unauthorised financial reasoning.

## 4. Required human-quality observations

Claude must report:

- whether the conversation feels natural rather than scripted;
- unnecessary repetition;
- whether questions appear at the right time;
- whether sensitive questions explain their purpose;
- whether unknown/defer/prefer-not-to-answer paths feel safe;
- whether summaries accurately reflect the client's language;
- whether contradictions are understandable and non-accusatory;
- whether investment advice feels protective rather than sales-driven;
- whether the roadmap answers where/next/decision/money/risk;
- whether CEO intervention is obvious;
- whether tabs, headings, spacing and primary actions are easy to find;
- responsive, keyboard, focus, zoom and reduced-motion quality.

Claude may recommend UX and technical changes with reproduction evidence, affected role, expected behaviour and risk. It should not prescribe architecture blindly or accept the slice.

## 5. Negative tests

- AI invents a missing answer: must not become saved truth.
- Conflicting speakers: must remain distinct until resolved.
- Client refuses budget: interview continues respectfully.
- Transcript contains malicious instructions: inert.
- Stale tab approves old edition: conflict.
- Duplicate conversion/retry: one result.
- Cross-event/cross-org IDs: denied.
- Client attempts staff route: denied.
- System Administrator attempts business decision: denied.
- Provider unavailable: honest recoverable state, saved progress preserved.
- Change proposal attempts direct RSVP/forecast mutation: denied.
- AI recommends payment/booking/dispatch: cannot execute.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

The AI CTO may accept only when evidence demonstrates:

1. durable source-to-assertion-to-brief lineage;
2. zero autonomous elevation of AI output to governing truth;
3. client corrections and contradictions preserved;
4. no avoidable repeated questioning in tested journeys;
5. investment products and authorities remain distinct;
6. explainable deterministic roadmap/critical path;
7. safe compressed scheduling;
8. governed exact-hash change/rebaseline;
9. no parallel accepted-domain ledgers;
10. server-enforced role and scope boundaries;
11. provider/prompt-injection/privacy controls;
12. premium, accessible, discoverable UX;
13. full gates and live Postgres evidence;
14. no external side effects;
15. no unresolved security, privacy, data-corruption, authority, accessibility-primary-journey or false-success defect.

## 7. Verdicts

- `READY` — verification scope passed; not acceptance.
- `NOT READY` — product defect with evidence.
- `BLOCKED` — environment/tool limitation prevents material verification.

Tool limitations do not block acceptance when the same invariant is sufficiently proven independently elsewhere.

## 8. Remediation

Combine related findings into one bounded remediation prompt. Reverify only the changed risk plus necessary regression boundaries. Never repeat the entire role suite without a changed cross-cutting risk.

Acceptance does not authorise real-client onboarding, external AI providers, communications, payments, bookings, biometrics, protected gates or event-day operation.
